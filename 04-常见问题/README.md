# A4 · Frequently Asked Questions on the Chinese Calendar

**[简体中文](README.zh.md) ｜ [繁體中文](README.zh-Hant.md) ｜ English ｜ [日本語](README.ja.md) ｜ [한국어](README.ko.md)**

## What this is

**197 questions and answers** on the Chinese calendar: leap months and the reckoning of the lunar year; the twenty-four solar terms; the phases of the Moon, eclipses and planetary phenomena; the sexagenary cycle; the twenty-eight lunar mansions; and the history of calendrical reform.

Each entry was first published on [kuangchujia.com](https://kuangchujia.com/) and is mirrored here as structured data in **five languages**.

## Terminology

The English edition follows the conventions established for the history of Chinese science — Needham's *Science and Civilisation in China* and the work of Nathan Sivin. **干支** is rendered **the sexagenary cycle**, **岁差** **the precession of the equinoxes**, **二十八宿** **the twenty-eight lunar mansions**. Romanised forms are given only where a term has no settled English equivalent.

The Japanese and Korean editions follow the same rule: the Japanese edition uses the usage of the National Astronomical Observatory of Japan for calendrical terms, annotating rare readings with `<ruby>`; the Korean edition writes core calendrical terms as Hangul followed by the Hanja in parentheses, so that homophones are not confused.
